# Interview Preparation Guide

## Preparation Tips

- **Research the Client:** Understand what they do, their values, and their industry. This helps you show interest and alignment. The client is Pinnacle Group (www.pinnacle1.com).
- **Review your experience.** Below are the suggested areas and questions you should prepare for:
  - **Behavioral Questions – Be Prepared to Talk About:**
    - Your career history (be concise; keep it under 5 minutes)
    - Handling feedback, pressure, and mistakes – use real examples
    - Your current role – projects, tools (SQL, SSIS, databases)
    - Likes/dislikes about your current job
    - Career goals – what you want to learn and your 2–3 year plan
    - Ideal work environment and what you seek in a new role
  - **Technical Areas to Review:**
    - **SQL (Very Important)**
      - Order of execution
      - Stored procedures, user-defined functions (scalar, table)
      - Temp tables (global, local) vs CTEs
      - Index: Cluster vs Non Cluster
      - Window functions (RANK, DENSE_RANK, ROW_NUMBER, PARTITION BY).
      - Subqueries and joins (INNER, OUTER, FULL OUTER, CROSS)
      - _Note: Make sure to practice the specific technical questions if you have them on "page 3"._
    - **ETL / SSIS / Data Flows**
      - Task types in SSIS (e.g., Data Flow, Execute SQL Task, etc.)
      - Data Flow Components: Sources (ADO NET, XML, Flat File), Transformations, Destinations.
      - Key Transformations: Conditional Split, Derived Column, Lookup, Pivot/Unpivot, Sort, Fuzzy Grouping.
      - Error Handling in Data Flows: Error outputs, row-level error redirection, ErrorCode and ErrorColumn.
      - Connection Managers and External Metadata.
      - End-to-end ETL data validation methods
      - SCD Types (especially Type 1 & 2)
    - **Data Modeling & Warehousing**
      - Schema types (Star, Snowflake, Galaxy)
      - Difference between OLAP and OLTP
    - **BI Tools**
      - Power BI & Tableau usage, dashboards, drilldowns, filters
      - Data cleansing, profiling, and visualization practices
- **Test your setup.** Make sure your internet, camera, and mic are working smoothly. Find a quiet, well-lit space.

---

## During the Interview

- **Be Calm, Professional and Polite:** Address the interviewer professionally and maintain a positive tone.

### Pro-Tip: Enable Live Captions (MS Teams)

If the interview is on Microsoft Teams and you find it helpful to read along, you can enable live captions:

1. Click the **More (...)** button at the top of the meeting window.
2. Hover over **Language and speech**.
3. Click on **Show live captions**.
