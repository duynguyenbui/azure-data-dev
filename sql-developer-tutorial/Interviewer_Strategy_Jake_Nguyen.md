# Interview Strategy: Jake Nguyen (BI Manager)

**Interviewer:** Jake Nguyen
**Role:** BI Manager
**Interview Focus:** As a BI Manager, Jake is likely looking for a mix of strong technical foundation, the ability to work independently, and an understanding of how data impacts business decisions. He will care deeply about code quality, process automation, and communication.

---

## 🎯 Key Traits Jake is Looking For

1.  **Ownership & Independence:** Managers love developers who don't need to be micromanaged. Emphasize your ability to take a requirement from start to finish.
2.  **Problem Solving & Technical Debt:** He will appreciate your experience working with legacy code at Ready Workforce and making it run more efficiently.
3.  **Communication Skills:** He needs someone who can talk to stakeholders, clarify requirements, and handle conflicts gracefully (use the communication strategies we practiced!).
4.  **Big Picture Thinking:** A BI Manager cares about the entire pipeline (ETL -> Data Warehouse -> SSAS -> Power BI) and ensuring data accuracy for the end-user.

---

## 💡 How to Tailor Your Answers for Jake

### When talking about Technical Skills (SQL, ETL, Azure):
*   **Don't just list technologies.** Talk about *impact*.
*   *Example:* "I don't just write complex SQL; I make sure my stored procedures are optimized so that the overnight ETL finishes before the US team logs in. I know how important data availability is to the business."

### When talking about Troubleshooting:
*   *Example:* "When a pipeline fails, my first priority is communication. I let the stakeholders know we are looking into it, find the root cause in the execution logs, deploy a fix, and then document it so the team knows how to handle it next time."

### When talking about Legacy Code:
*   *Example:* "I'm very comfortable working with older SQL codebases. At Ready Workforce, I often have to reverse-engineer legacy logic to optimize it or migrate it to newer structures without breaking the business rules."

---

## 🙋‍♂️ Smart Questions to Ask Jake at the End

Asking great questions shows a manager that you are already thinking about how you can add value to his team.

**1. About the Team's Current Challenges:**
> *"Jake, as the BI Manager, what would you say is the biggest data challenge your team is currently facing? Is it scaling the infrastructure, migrating to Azure, or handling complex business logic?"*

**2. About Success Metrics:**
> *"If I were to join your team, what does success look like for this role in the first 90 days?"*

**3. About the Cloud Migration (From JD):**
> *"I noticed the job description mentions both on-prem tools like SSIS and cloud tools like Azure Data Factory. Where is the team currently at in your cloud migration journey?"*

**4. About Team Dynamics:**
> *"How does the BI team collaborate with the US clients on a daily basis? Is it mostly direct communication, or do we work through business analysts?"*

---

## 🚀 Final Reminder for Meeting Jake
- **Be Conversational:** Treat him as a future colleague, not an interrogator.
- **Smile and Relax:** Show him you are friendly and easy to work with.
- **Use the Magic Words:** *Proactive, Ownership, End-to-end, Optimization, Clear Communication.*
