## WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING

We are seeking a skilled SQL Developer with at least 2 years of hands-on experience, capable of independently designing, planning, debugging, monitoring, and troubleshooting BI solutions.

**Main Responsibilities:**

- Own the design, development, and maintenance of ETLs and data pipelines.
- Ensure data availability and accuracy in the data warehouse.
- Monitor recurring processes and ensure output accuracy and timely distribution.
- Design, develop, and deploy an automated BI solution using **SQL Server, SSIS, SSAS, and Microsoft Azure**.
- Analyze data and create reports and dashboards.
- Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve data and reporting issues.
- Responsible for maintenance, documentation, and enhancements of recurring processes and procedures.

## WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

**Qualifications:**

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, or a related field required.
- **Advanced SQL knowledge and experience** (stored procedures, functions, triggers) and understanding of data warehousing and data modelling.
- Experienced with **Azure Data Warehousing, Data Factory, and Azure DevOps**.
- Basic **C#** or other programming languages (**Python**).
- Advanced knowledge of data systems and the ability to transform and shape large usable data sets.
- Excellent English communication skills.

**Work Behavior:**

- Ability to lead, plan, and manage in an entrepreneurial, team-oriented environment.
- Highly organized with strong project management skills, and a drive to meet organizational objectives; ability to manage multiple projects on interrelated timelines.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Demonstrate experience in getting things done in a dynamic, entrepreneurial environment.
- Demonstrate a high attention to detail in the analysis and reporting of data.
